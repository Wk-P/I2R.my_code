# v1.2.0

## Background

Analysis of the lt batch 210f932e(_bc) found two things:

1. Over 5,000,000 training steps, the training curve (episode AR) converged to a plateau within the first few hundred thousand steps and stayed flat (even slightly declined) until the end, so a large part of the 5M-step budget idles on the plateau without further gain.
2. The existing "Services Placed" curve comes from `info["services_placed"]`, which equals `self._step` in the env — since the action-masking env always forces every service onto some ECU (a fallback when no legal ECU exists, even if it causes a capacity/conflict violation), this counter always equals `M` regardless of the policy, so the curve sits flat on the `M` dashed line in every historical training plot and **does not show the real progress of "placing legally" during training**.

## Changes in this version

### 1. Training steps reduced to 2 million

`shared/training_steps_config.py`:
- `GLOBAL_TOTAL_STEPS` reduced from `5_000_000` to `2_000_000` (affects ppo/ppo_mask/ppo_lagrangian/ppo_opt).
- The explicit override for `dqn`/`ddqn` also goes from `3_000_000` to `2_000_000`.

Rationale: the training curve plateaus long before 5 million steps; further training gives no observable gain, so steps are reduced to save compute without affecting the observed model quality.

### 2. valid_placed curve added to the training plots (ppo_mask of eq/gt/lt)

`scenarios/{eq,gt,lt}/ppo_mask/run_all.py`:
- `P4Callback` gains an `episode_valid_placed` list recording `info["valid_placed"]` per episode (the true number of legal placements, excluding the forced-overflow fallback).
- The third subplot of `plot_training_curve()` gains a valid placed/ep curve (purple), and the old curve's label becomes "services placed/ep (incl. forced-overflow fallback)" to avoid misreading.
- The end-of-training log line gains `valid_placed(last50)=.../M`, distinct from the existing `placed(last50)`.
- `run_all_bc.py` reuses `P4Callback`/`plot_training_curve` from `run_all.py`, so it gets the same metrics; only its own training-finished log line adds the `valid_placed(last50)` output.

### Version number

- The default `VERSION` in `shared/paths.py` goes from `1.1.0` to `1.2.0`.

## Notes

- This version only changes ppo_mask's training-curve visualization and the global training steps; the reward structure, env logic and the training-curve recording of the other algorithms (ppo/ppo_lagrangian/ppo_opt/dqn/ddqn) are unchanged; the same valid_placed visualization for them needs the same patch in their callbacks/plotting code.
- After reducing the steps, rerun a full lt/eq/gt round and use the new curve to check whether valid_placed also converges early, to tell whether lt's low success rate is limited by training or by the policy's structure (e.g. fragmentation).

## 1. Basis (single)

| Item | Final basis |
|---|---|
| Variants | PPO / DQN / DDQN × U / L / M / R = 12; 3 scenarios × 3 seeds each = 108 runs of 2×10⁷ steps |

## 4. Errors corrected on 2026-11-22 (relative to 202611/draft)

1. **Gap definition (corrected 2026-11-23, most important)**: per the author's definition, AR, ILP AR and the gap are computed only on instances completed without violation. The original draft and main table (v6.0.0-merge) used all 5000 instances. Everything was recomputed from the same 108 runs: main table, Tables 5.2/5.3/5.6, the definition in 5.2, numbers in the text, `table_main.tex`, both gap figures, and the results summary. Rankings are unchanged; PPO-M 4.38% → 4.36%, strategy row means 6.00/7.01/10.86/17.90% → 5.99/7.06/10.97/17.53%. 5.5 "Sources of the gap" was rewritten accordingly. "Nine of the 12 variants" was also corrected to eight.
2. 5.5 "Main findings" "47.5× on the largest scenario": LT and GT have the same N·M, so LT is not the largest → "47.5× on LT".
3. 5.5 "Main findings" "most of the remaining gap is concentrated" on LT: LT is 47% of PPO-M's summed gap → "largest on LT", with the three numbers.
4. 5.5 "Offline vs. online cost" "break-even decreases as problem size grows": LT and GT have the same size but break-evens 5× apart → "the slower the ILP per instance, the sooner".
5. 5.3 and the results summary "ILP time grows with problem size", "LT, the largest scenario" → grows with the number of services M (LT is 4.7× GT).
6. "About 48× on LT" unified to 47.5×; Table 1.1 row order unified to U→L→M→R.

Training times (LT 3.6 h, EQ 3.2 h, GT 3.6 h) were checked against `logs/v6.0.5-PPO-hyparam/driver.log` (196–224 min per run, 3 runs per GPU).
